Welcome to **Kimi Embroidery Repair Tech Station** – a free, practical knowledge base built by and for embroidery machine professionals.

This site is maintained by a team of technicians with **over a decade of combined experience** in diagnosing, repairing, and maintaining industrial embroidery machines (including Tajima, Barudan, Happy, ZSK, and many others). Every guide, error code table, and maintenance tip you find here comes from **real‑world repair cases we have personally handled**, not from generic manuals.

**What you can explore here:**
- Step‑by‑step fault diagnosis and error‑code solutions
- Preventive maintenance schedules and illustrated tutorials
- Parts identification, adjustment parameters, and common wear‑and‑tear fixes
- Practical repair tips and industry updates

We understand that every minute of machine downtime costs you time and money. That is why we focus on **actionable, no‑fluff content** – so you can quickly get your equipment back to work.

**Need further help?** If your issue is not covered here, or you face a tricky problem, please reach out via the Facebook link at the bottom of this page. When contacting us, please provide your machine model, error message, and a brief description – we will do our best to offer diagnostic suggestions.

> *Kimi Repair Station – Every stitch counts.*
